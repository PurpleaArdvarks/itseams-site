Clean, long screenshots.

itSeams cuts away the clutter in a screenshot, stitches several into one long image, and captures whole web pages, all on your iPhone and iPad.

  • Cut out bars, empty space and anything you don't want, and redact what's private.
  • Stitch screenshots or a scrolling screen recording into one image.
  • Capture a whole web page as one long image.
  • Everything happens on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
