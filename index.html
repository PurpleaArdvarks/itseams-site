What it does

Clean Up

Spots status bars, empty space, sponsored posts and, on web pages, newsletter sign-ups. You review each suggestion; nothing changes until you tap Apply, and you can undo any step.

Stitch

Join overlapping screenshots, or a screen recording of a scrolling page, into one long image. itSeams shows where each pair was joined.

Web Page Capture Pro

Open a page in a private browser and save the whole thing as one long image. The browsing session is erased when you finish.

Redact

Cover names, codes and faces with a solid block, pixelate or blur. A solid block replaces the pixels completely.

Export

Save a new photo, split a long image for posts, or make a PDF. Location and device details are removed by default, and your originals are never changed.

Shortcuts

Clean your latest screenshot from Shortcuts or the Action Button without opening the app. Anything uncertain is left for you to review.

Private by design

Data Not Collected
