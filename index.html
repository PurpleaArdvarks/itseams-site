What it does

Clean Up

Spots status bars, empty space, sponsored posts and, on web pages, newsletter sign-ups. You review each suggestion; nothing changes until you tap Apply, and you can undo any step.

Stitch

Join overlapping screenshots, or a screen recording of a scrolling page, into one long image. itSeams shows where each pair was joined.

Web Page Capture Pro

Open a page in a private browser and save the whole thing as one long image. The browsing session is erased when you finish.

Redact

Cover names, codes and faces with a solid block, pixelation or blur. A solid block replaces the pixels completely.

Export

Save a new photo or split a long image for posts. With Pro, also short posts, full-screen stories and print-ready PDFs. Location and device details are removed by default, and your originals are never changed.

Shortcuts

Clean your latest screenshot from Shortcuts or the Action Button without opening the app. Anything uncertain is left for you to review.

Private by design

  • On your device. Suggestions, text recognition and every edit run on your iPhone or iPad. Your images never leave it unless you share them.
  • Redactions are permanent. Covered pixels are replaced in every export, and PDFs are flat images with no hidden text underneath.
  • Clean exports. Location and device details are removed by default, and your originals in Photos are never changed.
  • Private browsing. Web Page Capture keeps pages in memory and erases them when you close the page; cookies stay off unless you sign in or allow them in Settings. When you sign in, your password goes only to the site: itSeams doesn’t read or store it. The site may keep you signed in until you sign out there, and your capture keeps whatever the page showed.
  • Short-lived copies. A copied image stays on this device and clears from the clipboard after 10 minutes.
  • Nothing else. No account, no ads, no tracking or analytics, and no third-party code.
