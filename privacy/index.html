Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

itSeams collects no data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code. Your images are processed on your device and never sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • Your images and projects. Each image you open becomes a project so your work isn't lost. A project keeps a copy of the original until you delete it. Temporary files are deleted when you're done with them.
  • What itSeams learns. To make better suggestions, itSeams can remember the layout of sites and apps you edit. It stores sites, apps and on-screen words as codes, not names. You can turn learning off and erase it in Settings.
  • Saved pages. Bookmarks you star, and recent pages only if you turn that on (it's off by default). You can lock them with Face ID; Face ID is handled by your device, and itSeams never sees your face data.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and performance reports are kept on your device. They leave it only if you share them yourself.

Like other app data, this is included in your device backups (to iCloud or your computer) if you back up your device.

Photos

itSeams asks to read your photo library only if you choose to see your recent screenshots, and works without it. Saving adds a new photo. itSeams never changes or deletes photos in your library.

Web Page Capture

Web Page Capture uses a private browser. The pages you open, and any searches you type, go directly to those websites and to the search engine you choose in Settings, as in any browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies. itSeams doesn't send us what you browse.

  • Cookies are off unless you sign in to a site or turn them on in Settings.
  • The browsing session is erased when you finish, and after the time limit set in Settings.
  • Pages can't use your camera, microphone or location.

Sharing and copying

Images leave itSeams only when you save, copy or share them. A copied image stays on this device (it isn't offered to your other devices) and is cleared from the clipboard after 10 minutes.

Purchases

itSeams Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

itSeams collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If itSeams ever starts collecting any data, the policy and the App Store listing will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a security issue to report: support@itseams.app.
