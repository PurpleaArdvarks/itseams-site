Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

itSeams collects no data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code. Your images are processed on your device and never sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • Your images and projects. Each image you open becomes a project so your work isn't lost. A project keeps a copy of the original until you delete it. Temporary files are deleted when you're done with them.
  • What itSeams learns. To make better suggestions, itSeams remembers the layout of sites and apps you edit as fingerprints: short codes, not their names or the words on screen. It also keeps a rough measure of how ad-heavy each site you capture is, in the same fingerprint form, on this device only and outside your backups. You can turn learning off or erase it in Settings; deleting all your projects erases the measurements too.
  • Saved pages. Bookmarks you star, plus recent pages if you turn them on (they're off by default). You can lock them with Face ID; Face ID is handled by your device, and itSeams never sees your face data.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and performance reports are kept on your device. They leave it only if you share them yourself.

Like other app data, this is included in your device backups (to iCloud or your computer) if you back up your device.

Photos

itSeams asks to read your photo library only if you choose to see your recent screenshots; the app works without it. Saving adds a new photo. itSeams never changes or deletes photos in your library.

Web Page Capture

Web Page Capture uses a private browser. The pages you open, and any searches you type, go directly to those websites and to the search engine you choose in Settings, as in any browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies. itSeams doesn't send us what you browse.

  • Cookies are off unless you sign in to a site or turn them on in Settings.
  • The browsing session is erased when you finish, or when the time limit in Settings runs out.
  • Pages can't use your camera, microphone or location.

Signing in to websites. Web Page Capture can sign you in to a site for one capture. What you type there, including passwords and verification codes, goes directly to that site through Apple’s WebKit; itSeams doesn’t read, store or send it, and has no access to your Passwords app or iCloud Keychain. iOS may offer to fill in passwords you’ve saved there; that happens between you and iOS. The browser keeps cookies and site data in memory only and erases them when you close the page, turn sign-in off, or the sign-in time limit passes. The site you sign in to receives your sign-in under its own privacy policy and may keep you signed in until you sign out there. A capture is an image of what the page showed, which can include your name or other account details; it stays on your device until you edit or delete it. Keep your passwords in your Passwords app (iCloud Keychain) or your password manager, not in itSeams.

Sharing and copying

Images leave itSeams only when you save, copy or share them. A copied image stays on this device (it isn't offered to your other devices) and is cleared from the clipboard after 10 minutes.

Purchases

itSeams Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

itSeams collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If itSeams ever starts collecting any data, the policy and the App Store listing will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a security issue to report: support@itseams.app.
